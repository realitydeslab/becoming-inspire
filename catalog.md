## Becoming Mole

Touch-first and subterranean worlds: star-nosed moles, worms and soil life, and sight taken away so that touch can lead.

### Touch-first worlds

Experiences where haptics and touch replace vision as the main way of knowing.

#### FeltSight — Danlin Huang, Botao 'Amber' Hu, Reality Design Lab (2025)
- Type: Artwork · Senses: Touch & haptics, Altered vision · Medium: Mixed reality, Wearable & sensory device
- Shown at: SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 Art Papers; IEEE VIS 2025 Arts Program; ISMAR 2025 Demo; ACM CHI 2026 Interactivity; IEEE VR 2026 XR Gallery
- Idea: Becoming mole means reduced reality: sight withdraws and the world arrives through an extended reach of touch.
- What it is: A mixed-reality haptic glove modelled on the star-nosed mole: walking through a forest, participants feel nearby objects through their fingertips before they touch them, while the headset shows only faint point clouds that appear where the hand explores.
- How it works: Apple Vision Pro app linked by BLE to a silicone glove; LiDAR and hand tracking measure proximity, AI classifies materials, and per-fingertip actuators play audio-synthesised textures.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1145/3757369.3767605 (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 Art Papers)
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Pq6s3VnD0A
- Images: https://reality.design/media/feltsight-cover-forest-closeup.webp https://reality.design/media/feltsight-danlin-22.webp https://arxiv.org/html/2511.12533v3/images/feltsight.jpg
- Project page: https://danlinhuang.com/feltsight-1
- Code: https://github.com/realitydeslab/feltsight

## Becoming Bat

Echolocation and sonic worlds: hearing space like a bat or dolphin, infrasound, vibration and deep listening.

### Echolocation

Seeing with sound: bats, dolphins and human echolocation made perceptible.

#### EchoSense — Danyang Peng (2025)
- Type: Research prototype · Senses: Echolocation, Touch & haptics · Medium: VR headset, Wearable & sensory device
- Idea: Biomimetic empathy: understand an animal by borrowing its way of sensing.
- What it is: A frontal haptic display that lets VR users feel the space in front of them as an echolocating animal might, while navigating without sight.
- How it works: Wearable vibrotactile array on the torso or face driven by distances in the virtual scene (details likely).
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1145/3714394.3754428 (UbiComp/ISWC 2025 Companion)

#### BATOPIA — Yiou Wang (2024)
- Type: Artwork · Senses: Echolocation, Hearing & vibration · Medium: VR headset, Game
- Shown at: :iidrr Gallery New York 2024; MIT Museum 2024; ISEA 2025
- Idea: Echolocation is active sensing: you only perceive the world by calling into it, and human noise erases that world.
- What it is: A first-person VR adventure in which you live as a bat, foraging at night through a city by calling and listening, while noise and light pollution disturb your hunt.
- How it works: Built in Unreal Engine for Meta Quest with bat field recordings, ultrasound-derived sound design and a custom bat headset mask; participants' calls reveal the scene visually and sonically.
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1XMN4u9jMQ
- Images: https://yiouwang.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/BATOPIA_Human_to_Bat.jpg https://yiouwang.org/wp-content/uploads/Batopia_game_scene_52-1024x542.png

#### BatSight — Samira Poudratchi (2024)
- Type: Research prototype · Senses: Echolocation, Hearing & vibration · Medium: Wearable & sensory device, Game, AR
- Idea: Play as a bat: navigate a real space by ear alone.
- What it is: An audio game in which blindfolded players walk through a physical maze guided by a sonar headset that turns obstacles into musical sounds.
- How it works: Head-mounted ultrasonic distance sensors mapped to musical cues over headphones, in an AR-style physical maze.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.17083/ijsg.v11i1.718 (International Journal of Serious Games 2024)

#### EchoVision — Botao 'Amber' Hu, Jiabao Li, Danlin Huang, Reality Design Lab (2024)
- Type: Artwork · Senses: Echolocation, Hearing & vibration, Altered vision · Medium: Mixed reality, Multisensory installation
- Shown at: SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 Art Papers & XR; UbiComp/ISWC 2024 Design Exhibition; IEEE VIS 2024 Arts Program; West Bund Art Festival 2024; TANK Art Festival 2024; The Contemporary Austin / Fusebox 2024; SXSW 2025 XR Experience; IEEE VR 2025 XR Gallery; ACM CHI 2025 Interactivity; Augmented Humans 2025 Best Demo Award; CURRENTS New Media 2025; Plásmata 3, Onassis Stegi, Athens 2025

#### What is it like to be a (virtual) bat? — Zheng Mahler (2023)

#### Echolocation-Enabled Virtual Environments — Ronny Andrade (2022)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Echolocation, Hearing & vibration · Medium: VR headset, Spatial audio, Game
- Idea: People who already echolocate are the best designers of echolocation in VR.
- What it is: Expert blind echolocators helped design and test virtual environments that can be explored by clicking and listening to echoes.
- How it works: Participatory design with focus groups and two prototype iterations of acoustic ray-traced echoes in a game engine.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1145/3516448 (ACM Transactions on Accessible Computing 2022)

#### Auditory Feedback for Navigation with Echoes — Anastassia Andreasen (2019)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Echolocation, Hearing & vibration · Medium: VR headset, Spatial audio
- Idea: Echolocation can be learned in VR as a navigation skill, not only simulated as an effect.
- What it is: A training procedure in which sighted people learn to navigate a virtual maze by echoes alone, and the orientation strategies they develop.
- How it works: Real-time acoustic rendering of self-produced clicks and their reflections in a virtual maze over headphones.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1109/tvcg.2019.2898787 (IEEE TVCG 2019 (IEEE VR 2019))
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8ndSJDbSok

#### What Is It Like to Be a Virtual Bat? — Anastassia Andreasen (2018)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Echolocation, Body schema & movement · Medium: VR headset
- Idea: Nagel's question turned into a design brief: combine a bat body with bat hearing.
- What it is: A VR system in which the participant has a bat's body, flies with their arms as wings and navigates a dark cave by emitting calls and hearing echoes.
- How it works: Head-mounted display with arm tracking for wing flapping and real-time spatial audio echoes rendered with HRTFs.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-06134-0_57 (ArtsIT 2018 (LNICST 2019))
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uB2ApqoNzzU

#### Bat-Modelled Sonar in Virtual Environments — Dean A. Waters (2007)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Echolocation, Hearing & vibration · Medium: VR headset, Spatial audio
- Idea: Early proof that bat-style echo processing can guide human movement in VR.
- What it is: A bat biologist's model of bat sonar used as a navigation tool for people moving through virtual environments, following his 2001 'virtual bat'.
- How it works: Synthetic frequency-modulated calls and echoes computed from the virtual geometry, played binaurally (details from abstract-level sources; likely desktop VR).
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhcs.2007.06.001 (International Journal of Human-Computer Studies 2007)

#### Darker Than Night — Eduardo Kac (1999)
- Type: Artwork · Senses: Echolocation, Hearing & vibration · Medium: VR headset, Multisensory installation
- Shown at: Fables of a Technological Era, Blijdorp Zoo Rotterdam 1999
- Idea: Humans enter the bats' space as another sonar-emitting body, and bat and human signals meet in the same frequency band.
- What it is: A telepresence work in the bat cave of Rotterdam Zoo: wearing a VR headset, visitors took the point of view of a robotic bat hanging among 300 Egyptian fruit bats and saw the cave through its sonar.
- How it works: A 'batbot' with a 45 kHz sonar unit and motorised head swivelled with the headset; returns were visualised in the headset and bat calls were made audible by frequency conversion.
- Images: https://www.ekac.org/darker_than_night_batcave.jpg https://www.ekac.org/darker.vrheadset.jpg
- Project page: https://www.ekac.org/darker-info.html

### Bats & night life

Works about bats and nocturnal animals as beings, beyond their sense.

#### Agency and the Virtual Bat Body — Anastassia Andreasen (2018)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Body schema & movement · Medium: VR headset
- Idea: Owning a bat body depends on being the one who flies it.
- What it is: Participants flew as a virtual bat under four conditions, from full control of wings and movement to passive, involuntary flight.
- How it works: Head-mounted VR with arm-tracked wings; within-subject comparison of voluntary and involuntary locomotion.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1109/vr.2018.8446448 (IEEE VR 2018 (poster))

#### Touching the Wings of a Virtual Bat — Anastassia Andreasen (2018)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Touch & haptics, Body schema & movement · Medium: VR headset
- Idea: A wing is not an arm: touch can be remapped across different anatomy, up to a point.
- What it is: While seeing an object touch a bat's wing, participants felt a touch on their arm at matching or increasingly shifted positions.
- How it works: Visuo-tactile stimulation with 0%, 50% and 70% spatial deviation between real arm and virtual wing touch points.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1109/vr.2018.8446569 (IEEE VR 2018 (poster))

## Becoming Fish

Aquatic life: fish, whales and dolphins, coral, jellyfish and plankton, and the lateral line, buoyancy and pressure of water.

### Fish

Swimming, schooling and sensing as a fish: lateral line, electroreception, underwater vision.

#### Black Wings — Lai Guan-yuan (2024)
- Type: Artwork · Senses: Body schema & movement, Hearing & vibration, Altered vision · Medium: Mixed reality, Multisensory installation
- Shown at: Kaohsiung Film Festival XR Dreamland 2024 (Taiwan Wave: Black Current)
- Idea: Becoming the most sacred flying fish of the Tao, through reading: your voice moves the ocean around you.
- What it is: A mixed-reality 'embodied reading' of Tao writer Syaman Rapongan's novel: the visitor reads passages aloud from a physical book and becomes a black-winged flying fish travelling the ancient Kuroshio current, fighting predators, while interviews with the author frame the journey.
- How it works: MR headset with speech recognition: the reader's voice triggers changes in the projected environment and is processed by AI into ambient sound.
- Images: https://images.vocus.cc/44071258-56ab-43f3-8759-2a9d3d17ee4f.png https://images.vocus.cc/caa55f68-9c54-40cd-93fe-cc9235119625.png

#### JeL — John Desnoyers-Stewart (2019)
- Type: Research prototype · Senses: Breath & interoception, Collective & networked · Medium: VR headset, Multisensory installation
- Shown at: CHI 2019 Late-Breaking Work
- Idea: Breathing as a jellyfish, and with another person, turns pulsing propulsion into a shared rhythm with the reef.
- What it is: A two-person VR installation set underwater, where each person's breathing moves a jellyfish, and breathing in sync grows a glass-sponge structure between them.

#### The Stanford Ocean Acidification Experience — Stanford Virtual Human Interaction Lab (2016)
- Type: Research prototype · Senses: Body schema & movement, Time & scale · Medium: VR headset

- How it works: Oculus Rift DK2 or HTC Vive; narration tells users they are coral, and looking down shows a coral avatar; studied in classroom trials.

## Becoming Octopus

Distributed and extended bodies: cephalopods, tentacles and tails, supernumerary limbs, shared and collective bodies.

### Cephalopods

Octopus, squid and cuttlefish: distributed nervous systems, camouflage, arms that think.

#### Embodied Tentacle — Shuto Takashita (2024)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Body schema & movement · Medium: VR headset
- Idea: A tentacle cannot be copied from an arm; the mapping itself must be designed.
- What it is: Participants controlled an unbranched 12-joint, octopus-like virtual arm with their own arm, comparing mappings for reaching and wrapping tasks.
- How it works: Head-mounted VR with arm and hand tracking mapped to 12 tentacle joints through several mapping methods.
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642340 (CHI 2024)
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41AYcDhbT0E

#### Non-human Looking Robot Arms Induce Illusion of Embodiment — Laura Aymerich-Franch (2017)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Body schema & movement, Altered vision · Medium: VR headset
- Idea: A body does not have to look human to become yours; moving it and seeing from it is enough.
- What it is: Participants felt embodied in a humanoid robot even when its arms ended in mechanical grippers rather than human-like hands.

#### The Second Me: Humanoid Robot Embodiment and Bi-location — Laura Aymerich-Franch (2016)
- Type: Paper · Senses: Body schema & movement, Altered vision · Medium: VR headset
- Idea: Being a robot does not erase the human body: the self can split between the machine and the flesh.
- What it is: People embodied in an HRP-2 humanoid robot saw their own real body through the robot's eyes, and felt as if they were in two places at once.
- How it works: Participants wore a head-mounted display showing the HRP-2's head camera view while controlling the robot; questionnaires measured embodiment and bi-location when their real body appeared in view.

- What it is: Participants moved a pair of android hands only by imagining the movement, and came to feel the robot hands as their own.
- How it works: An EEG motor-imagery brain-computer interface drove the hands of a Geminoid android seen from a first-person view (likely through a head-mounted display); ownership was measured by questionnaire and skin conductance.
